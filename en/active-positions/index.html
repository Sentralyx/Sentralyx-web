How does it work?

Every moment of a position, by rules

From open to close, protections work according to your settings.

01 · Open

A signal arrives, a position opens

When a signal arrives, a position opens in virtual or real mode according to your settings. In real mode, SL and TP orders are placed on the exchange at the same time.

  • Spot only, hybrid or full futures mode
  • Leverage options 1x, 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x and 20x
  • No position opens if the free balance is not enough
02 · Manage

One-click actions

From the panel on the right you can close the selected position, close half of it or change its protection.

  • Close position and close half
  • Move SL to market, limit SL and set TP
  • Move SL to entry and close all
03 · Protect

Protect profit and limit risk

Trailing stop and break-even settings try to protect profit; the daily loss limit, maximum number of positions and maximum duration limit risk.

  • Positions close automatically when time runs out
  • If the exchange rejects a new stop, the previous stop is kept
  • Records are regularly checked against the exchange
Features

What's in position management?

Protection orders in the app and on the exchange.

SL/TP on the exchange

Stop and take-profit orders are placed on the exchange and stay there even when the app is closed.

Trailing stop

The stop level follows the price as it moves in your favour.

Break-even

Moving the stop to the entry price reduces the risk of a winning trade turning into a loss.

Time and limits

Maximum position duration, maximum open positions and daily loss limit.

Only its own positions

Works with "SX"-prefixed orders; does not touch another bot's or your own positions on the same account.

Position checks

Positions already closed on the exchange are closed in the records, and missing protection is restored where possible.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do SL and TP work when the app is closed?

In real mode, SL and TP orders are also sent to the exchange, so they remain there when the app is closed.

Does it touch positions I opened manually?

No. Sentralyx manages only the positions it opened and its own "SX"-prefixed orders.

Does a stop always fill at the exact price?

No. During sudden price moves, stop orders can fill at a worse price due to slippage.
