How does it work?

Your own trading system in three steps

No coding needed. Build your strategy, test it on history and in virtual mode, then run it automatically.

01 · Build

Build your strategy by choosing indicators

In the strategy wizard you choose the strategy type, market regime and indicators. You define the BUY/SELL rule for each indicator.

  • Scoring, Majority, Strong Majority and Full Consensus
  • ADX, ATR and market regime filters
  • Editable rules for 30 indicators
02 · Test

Try it before using real money

The backtest runs your strategy on historical data including commission and slippage. Then you try it in virtual mode in today's market without real money.

  • Commission and slippage simulation
  • Monte Carlo, day/hour/direction analysis, equity chart
  • Virtual mode: same flow, no real orders
03 · Run

From signal to position, by rules

When a signal arrives, a position opens according to your settings. Manage open positions from one screen: close, close half, move SL, set TP.

  • Spot only, hybrid or full futures mode
  • Daily loss limit and maximum number of positions
  • Manages only the positions it opened

Security

Your keys, your control

Sentralyx is a desktop application that runs on your computer. Measures designed to reduce risk:

Encrypted API keys

Keys are encrypted with a key derived from your device's hardware ID and stored locally. A settings file copied to another device cannot be decrypted.

No withdrawal permission needed

The app recommends keeping the withdrawal permission disabled on your API key. Read and trade permissions are enough.

SL/TP stay on the exchange

Stop and take-profit orders are also sent to the exchange and remain there even when the app is closed.

Only its own orders

Works with "SX"-prefixed orders; it does not touch another bot's or your own positions on the same account.

No. Sentralyx does not sell or distribute signals. Signals are produced by the strategy and rules you build.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You build your strategy in the strategy wizard by choosing indicators and edit BUY/SELL rules in the interface.

Does the free version open trades automatically?

No. The Free version scans and analyzes but does not open positions. You can try every feature for free with the 7-day Trial.

Are my API keys safe?

Keys are encrypted with a key derived from your device's hardware ID and stored locally. Keeping the withdrawal permission disabled on your API key is recommended.
