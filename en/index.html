Sentralyx

Algorithmic trading software that scans crypto markets 24/7.

Sentralyx continuously monitors the market, evaluates opportunities based on defined strategy rules, and turns the trading process into a more systematic workflow.

What does it do?

  • Automatically scans crypto markets.
  • Detects trade opportunities that match strategy conditions.
  • Manages entry, stop-loss, and target levels in a systematic way.
  • Aims to reduce the need to manually watch charts for hours.
  • Applies risk and position management according to defined rules.

Who is it for?

Sentralyx is built for users who want to trade crypto markets more systematically, connect their decisions to clear rules, and avoid constantly monitoring the market manually.

A real product

Sentralyx is not just an idea or a signal group. It is an actively developed, independent desktop trading application.
