Refund Policy

Digital product refund and cancellation conditions
Last updated: 25.08.2026
Sentralyx is a digital software product delivered electronically and activated with a license.
Cancellation and Refund Rights
Once the digital license has been delivered to the user and activated, the right of withdrawal and standard refund does not apply due to the nature of the product.
Exceptional Cases
The following situations are evaluated separately:
  • Duplicate or incorrect payment
  • License not delivered by Sentralyx
  • License unusable due to a verified technical issue caused by Sentralyx, which could not be resolved through the support process
In these cases, the user may request a review through support channels. If deemed appropriate following the review, a refund may be processed.
Exclusions
Issues arising from the user's device, internet connection, third-party services, exchange/API services, or other conditions outside of Sentralyx are not considered Sentralyx-caused technical issues.
Contact
For refund and technical issues: support@sentralyx.com
