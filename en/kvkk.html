Personal Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

This notice explains how Sentralyx handles personal data within its web experience and product ecosystem.

Policy: Informational Summary

1) Scope

This page is provided as an English summary for user clarity. If you require the official Turkish wording, refer to the Turkish KVKK document on the root page.

2) Data We Process

  • Contact Data: name and email when you contact support.
  • Technical Telemetry (Web): basic browser metadata required for safe operation (e.g., language preference).
  • Product Data (Local-First): strategy settings and sensitive credentials are designed to remain on the user’s device where applicable.

3) Security Credentials & Encrypted Storage

Sensitive fields (e.g., exchange API credentials) should be stored using encryption mechanisms. We recommend strict exchange-side permissions, IP whitelisting, and never enabling withdrawal permissions for trading keys.

4) Purpose of Processing

  • Providing support and responding to business inquiries.
  • Maintaining platform integrity and preventing abuse.
  • Delivering operational notifications based on user configuration.

5) Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or to meet legal obligations.

6) Your Rights

You may request information, correction, deletion, and other rights defined by KVKK. For requests, contact: support@sentralyx.com.
