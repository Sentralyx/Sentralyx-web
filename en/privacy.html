1. What Data Do We Process?

Sentralyx is designed with a "Privacy-First" principle. The following limited data is processed for technical necessity of system operation:

Important Note: Sentralyx never sends logs and connection data created for user's trading security to a central server; all technical records are stored only on the user's own machine.

Data Type Processing Purpose Legal Basis
HWID (Hardware ID) License verification and preventing unauthorized use Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6/1-b)
Telegram ID License delivery and technical support channel management Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6/1-b)
Email Address Update announcements and license notifications Consent (GDPR Art. 6/1-a)

2. Critical Security and Data Privacy

IMPORTANT SECURITY WARNING

Sentralyx NEVER sends your API keys, trading strategies, or trading history to its own servers or shares with third parties. This data is stored entirely on the user's local machine with Fernet and HWID-based encryption, and the data controller has no access to this data.

Your data is securely stored only on your disk with Atomic Write operation, while Thread Watchdog system protects the integrity of this data 24/7.

Limited Sharing Situations:
  • Hosting/Infrastructure: GitHub Inc. (GitHub Pages) infrastructure is used for website hosting (Only basic access logs).
  • Communication: Telegram infrastructure is used for license activation and support operations.
  • Legal Obligation: In case of a properly submitted request by authorized judicial authorities.

3. Data Retention Periods

  • License Data: During license validity period and 1 year following expiration.
  • Support Records: 2 years from resolution of technical support request.
  • System Logs: Maximum 6 months for debugging and security tracking.
  • IP Addresses: 1 year for security policy requirements.

4. Your Rights and Contact

Under GDPR Article 15; you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information if processed, to request correction if it is incomplete or incorrectly processed.

You can send your requests to destek@sentralyx.com address. Your applications will be finalized within 30 days at the latest.

5. Cookie Policy

Our website only uses technically mandatory cookies for system operation. Third-party advertising or tracking cookies are not used.
