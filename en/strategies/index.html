How does it work?

You set the rules

There are no ready-made signals; the strategy combines the results of the indicators you choose into a signal.

01 · Create

Strategy wizard

Choose the strategy name, type and market regime. Turn the trend strength (ADX) and volatility (ATR) filters on or off.

  • Scoring, Majority, Strong Majority, Full Consensus
  • Market regime: any, trend, sideways, volatile
  • Quick strategy option
02 · Pick indicators

Choose indicators by group

Indicators are grouped into bands/channels, crossovers, moving averages and oscillators. You edit each indicator's BUY/SELL rule on the Indicator screen.

  • Bollinger, Keltner, EMA, SMA, MACD and more
  • Separate rules for every indicator
  • Edit or delete strategies
03 · Evaluate

What makes a good strategy

The app includes reminders about good strategies: few but consistent rules, risk management, and not relying on a backtest result alone.

  • Do not expect the same result in every market
  • Too many filters can also reduce opportunities
  • The final decision and responsibility are yours
Features

What's on the strategy screen?

No coding, rules in plain sight.

Scoring System

Each indicator is weighted; a signal forms when the total score passes the threshold.

Majority Vote

Triggers when most indicators signal in the same direction.

Strong Majority

A majority with a higher threshold; more selective signals.

Full Consensus

All indicators must signal in the same direction.

Market regime

The strategy produces signals only in the conditions you choose.

Quick strategy

Quick creation, editing and deletion.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to code to build a strategy?

No. You choose the strategy type, regime, filters and indicators in the interface.

Which strategy type is best?

There is no single right answer. Full Consensus gives fewer but more selective signals, Majority gives more flexible signals. Comparing them with backtests and virtual mode is the healthiest way.

Does it come with ready-made strategies?

Sentralyx does not sell ready-made signals; you set the rules. You can start by editing the strategies in the app.
