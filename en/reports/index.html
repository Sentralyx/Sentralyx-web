Split results by day, see the trend

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I export reports to?

CSV, PDF and Excel.

Do reports separate real and virtual trades?

Reports summarize the trade history of the mode you use. Virtual results are not real trading results.

Do reports show future performance?

No. Reports summarize past trades and do not guarantee future results.
