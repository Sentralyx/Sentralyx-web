FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I export my trade history?

Yes. You can export closed positions as CSV; the Reports screen also offers CSV, PDF and Excel.

What is the close reason for?

It shows whether a trade closed by stop loss or because time ran out, helping you understand how your strategy and risk settings behave.

Are past results an indicator of the future?

No. Past results are not an indicator of future results.
